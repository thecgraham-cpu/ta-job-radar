# HirePilot Vision

## Mission

Help every job seeker spend less time searching and more time interviewing by providing an AI career agent that never stops working.

---

## The Problem

Today's job search is fragmented.

Candidates search dozens of websites, manually research companies, tailor resumes, track applications in spreadsheets, and often miss opportunities because jobs disappear before they find them.

Existing tools help organize applications or automate submissions, but very few actually help users make better career decisions.

---

## Our Vision

HirePilot becomes the world's smartest AI career agent.

Rather than acting like another job board, HirePilot continuously works in the background by:

- Discovering opportunities
- Researching companies
- Understanding each user's experience
- Prioritizing opportunities
- Explaining recommendations
- Helping users prepare and apply

Users shouldn't spend hours searching.

HirePilot should tell them exactly what deserves their attention.

---

## T.A.C.O.S.

Talent Acquisition Career Operating System

T.A.C.O.S. is the intelligence engine powering HirePilot.

Responsibilities include:

- Job Discovery
- Data Normalization
- Deduplication
- Resume Intelligence
- Company Intelligence
- Opportunity Scoring
- Recommendation Generation
- Notifications

---

## Long-Term Goal

Build the most intelligent AI-powered career platform in the world.

Instead of searching for jobs, people simply ask:

"What should I do next?"

HirePilot provides the answer.
