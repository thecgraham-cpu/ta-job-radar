# HirePilot Roadmap

## Vision

Build the world's most intelligent AI-powered career agent.

The roadmap is organized around capabilities rather than release versions. Every milestone should increase a user's probability of receiving interviews while reducing the time spent searching for opportunities.

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation

Establish the technical and product foundation for HirePilot and T.A.C.O.S.

Goals:
- Product vision
- System architecture
- Documentation
- Job discovery engine
- Core data models

---

# Phase 2 — Job Discovery

Discover opportunities faster and more reliably than traditional job boards.

---

# Phase 3 — Company Intelligence

Build reusable company profiles that help users evaluate opportunities.

---

# Phase 4 — Opportunity Intelligence

Score, rank, explain, and prioritize opportunities.

---

# Phase 5 — AI Career Agent

Provide personalized recommendations and next-best actions.

---

# Phase 6 — Growth

Expand integrations, notifications, and user experience.

---

# Phase 7 — Platform

Enable APIs, third-party integrations, enterprise features, and long-term scalability.
