# T.A.C.O.S.

**Talent Acquisition Career Operating System**

## Purpose

T.A.C.O.S. is the intelligence engine that powers HirePilot.

Its job is to discover opportunities, understand users, research companies, prioritize opportunities, and recommend the next best action.

The engine contains no user interface.

Every application—including the web app, mobile app, browser extension, API, and notifications—communicates with T.A.C.O.S.

---

## Core Philosophy

The goal of T.A.C.O.S. is not to find jobs.

The goal is to understand the hiring market better than the user does.

Every recommendation should increase the user's probability of receiving an interview.

---

## Primary Question

"What should this user do next?"
